01 Project Background & Scope +

Orange County Sanitation District's headquarters serves as a hub for teams coordinating essential public operations. EIDIM's AV integration supports collaboration, information sharing and staff communication across the new facility.

The project required an integrated audiovisual environment across a range of meeting, training and collaboration spaces, while coordinating installation activity with construction and other contractors.

02 Installation Challenges +

The construction project required close coordination with electrical contractors, network teams and facility personnel. Installation schedules, room needs and design requirements evolved as work progressed.

Many environments required different equipment, connectivity and room-specific tuning, while the completed systems still had to operate as a coherent, dependable environment.

03 Audio Capture & Processing +

Microphones: Shure MXA920 ceiling array, MXA710 linear array and other KKW wireless microphone systems were selected for clear speech pickup and discreet installation.

Processing: Q-SYS Core processors support audio routing, echo cancellation, noise reduction, room equalization and tuning within the integrated audio system.

04 Video Conferencing & Distribution +

Conferencing: Poly G7500 systems enable hybrid collaboration in larger meeting rooms, pairing conferencing capabilities with room cameras and microphones.

AV transport: Networked video encoders and decoders distribute signals to the appropriate displays and endpoints, simplifying connectivity and system control.

05 Presentation Systems & Outcome +

Displays: Samsung commercial displays support content sharing, presentations and meeting-room collaboration. Integrated room control helps staff switch between presentation and conferencing modes.

Outcome: The completed headquarters provides a coordinated AV environment supporting communication, public service and daily operational needs, with systems designed for future maintenance and upgrades.
